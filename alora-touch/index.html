Our Story

Born from a
Love of Craft

Alora Touch was founded on the belief that modesty and luxury are not opposites — they are complements. Each abaya in our collection tells a story of devotion: to craft, to detail, and to the women who wear them.

We source the finest fabrics from across the world and work with skilled artisans to bring each design to life — one stitch at a time.
